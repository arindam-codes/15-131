> **Difficulty Philosophy**
>
> This laboratory follows the **CS50 model**.
>
> Vim is not taught by memorizing commands.
>
> Instead, you are placed into a realistic production incident where efficient modal editing is the only practical solution.
>
> The objective is to develop intuition through repeated mistakes, debugging, and progressively improving editing efficiency rather than simply learning Vim commands.
